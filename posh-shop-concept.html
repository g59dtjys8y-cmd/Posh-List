Version one

  • Link opens the listNo install, no password, no support calls.
  • Live sync, who added whatEnds the "did you already get milk" text.
  • Your store's aisle orderEditable once, then it just matches the walk.
  • Regulars, one tap back onBin bags and coffee shouldn't need retyping every month.
  • Works with no signalTicking off in the aisles has to survive a dead spot.

Once it's actually being used

  • Voice addHands in the sink — "add kitchen roll."
  • Recipe → listWhere Posh Nosh could eventually feed this.
  • A second listButcher, DIY, Christmas.

Not building

  • Accounts and profilesThe thing that kills household adoption.
  • Prices and spend trackingTurns a 5-second task into data entry.
  • Meal plans, chores, calendarA different app wearing this one as a hat.
